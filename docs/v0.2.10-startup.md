# V0.2.10 — WireGuard startup and ICE transport hardening

## ICE transport replacement

`ICEBind` assigns a monotonic **generation** per VPN peer. `readLoop` and `Send` ignore superseded transports so a replaced connection cannot deliver stale packets or race map updates.

## WireGuard handshake after ICE attach

Peers are configured at daemon start; until `SetPeerConn`, wireguard-go may hit `ErrNoTransport`. After ICE nomination the daemon calls `Engine.NudgeHandshake` once to send a handshake initiation without arbitrary sleeps.

Diagnostics record **ICE recovery** (transport ready) separately from **VPN recovery** (WireGuard handshake / data path).

## Credential-timeout races

`pendingSignals` already queues `ice_credentials` before a session handler exists. Remaining deploy races occur when the remote daemon has not processed `session_ready` yet; initiators now wait up to 12s for the peer to appear **online** on the coordination API before `CreateSession`.
